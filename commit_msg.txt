Add FPGA implementation flow, DSP-friendly variant, and TCAS-II paper draft

Implementation (Vivado 2023.2, xc7a100tcsg324-1, out of context, 4.0 ns):

- Add pipelined_mac_zs_dsp.v: the input-registered zero-skip MAC with no
  datapath reset. DSP48E1 A/B/M pipeline registers accept only synchronous
  reset, so the previous async-reset style stranded every datapath register in
  fabric (AREG=BREG=MREG=0). Removing it gives AREG=BREG=MREG=PREG=8 and lets
  the accumulator carry chain fold into the DSP's own adder: 530 LUT / 328 FF
  / 63 CARRY4 / 283.0 MHz, against 789 / 546 / 127 / 269.8 MHz for the ungated
  baseline. tb_dsp_equiv.v proves the two variants bit-identical over 3031
  cycles and 2823 result pulses.

- Add mac_cascade_dsp_nogate_top as a structural control, so the cost of the
  gating can be separated from the cost of the extra pipeline stage and the
  reset-style change. Against that control the gating costs +117 LUT and
  +30 FF with no frequency penalty.

- Add synth/: non-project build, SAIF power and netlist scripts, plus the
  utilization, timing and power reports. synth/README.md documents the flow,
  what in rpt/ is trustworthy, and two bugs found and fixed along the way (a
  false_path on input ports that silently excluded the two-stage designs'
  multiplier-input paths from timing analysis; and cmd FOR+CALL variable
  scoping that ran sweep points at the wrong sparsity while labelling them
  correctly).

- Add paper/: TCAS-II Express Brief draft (IEEEtran, 4 pages), figure and
  table generators, and the underlying measured data.

Housekeeping:

- Relicense from proprietary to Apache-2.0 ahead of release with the paper.
- Add .gitignore and untrack compiled simulation binaries and Vivado
  checkpoints (~25 MB of regenerable artefacts).
- Delete the powerpost_* reports: that post-implementation SAIF attempt did
  not improve net matching and several of its runs were mislabelled, so the
  limitation is documented rather than the bad data retained.
