Add architecture figure to the paper (Fig. 1)

The brief previously had no block diagram, which is unusual for a hardware
paper and left readers to reconstruct the split-enable structure from prose
and equations alone.

Adds paper/fig_arch.tex, a full-width two-panel TikZ figure:

  (a) the cascade - array and adder tree each own an independent
      completion-aware gate sized to their own depth, with no central
      controller, and the update bus shown as the only wire the sparsity
      extension adds between stages;

  (b) one lane - ce_ctrl never masked and triggered by the raw valid, so the
      drain counter is structurally blind to sparsity, while ce_mult and
      ce_acc gate only the two wide register banks, and the skip decision
      rides with its own item as skip_p.

Both panels are referenced from the text (Sections II and IV-A). The paper
remains 5 pages, exactly the TCAS-II limit, with no overfull boxes.
